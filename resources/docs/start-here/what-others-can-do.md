> [!why] What you can do follows from your billet on the Cav roster. If a button is missing for you, it belongs to someone above you, and this page says who.

## Leaders

A leadership billet gives you the unit it commands and everything under it. A company commander has the same rights as the platoon and section leaders in their company, on every unit in it.

| Who | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Section Leader, Assistant Section Leader | Their section's rollcall, section practice marks, guests, fireteams, weekly report and its own rollcall templates |
| Platoon Leader, Platoon Sergeant | All of that for every section in the platoon, the platoon's report and its training topic |
| Company command (Commander, XO, First Sergeant) | All of that for the company, its report, the company's fireteam rules and its rollcall templates |
| Battalion and regiment command | All of that for their battalion, or for the regiment |
| Acting lead | What the billet would give, for the unit an Admin assigned them |

## Staff and everyone else

| Who | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Staff grant holder | Read a unit an Admin gave them access to. Never change anything there. |
| Regimental HQ staff (Chief of Staff, Adjutants General, Finance Officer and the rest of Regimental HQ) | Admins, once an Admin approves their billet. Until then, those placed on the regiment read the whole regiment without a grant and change nothing through it. |
| S6 HQ staff | Admins, once an Admin approves their billet. Until then, what their billet in the S6 department gives them. |
| Trooper with no command billet | Read the docs, their own account and their own milpac page, with their own counseling records, read only. Staff record the practices they attended there. Anything else needs a billet or a grant. |

## Admins

Admins are the people given the Admin flag, and Regimental HQ or S6 HQ staff whose billet an Admin approved. Admins see and change everything. Only they edit the org structure, staff grants, the regiment default rollcall template and games, read the audit log, and assign acting leads.

## Who to ask

- Wrong rank, billet or unit: S1, since it comes from your milpac.
- A button you should have and do not: your chain of command, then S6.
- Access for a staff role: an Admin, for a staff grant.
