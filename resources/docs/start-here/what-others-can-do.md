> [!why] Nobody is given access by hand. What you can do follows from your billet on the Cav roster, so if a button is missing for you, it belongs to someone above you, and this page says who.

## Leaders

A leadership billet gives you the unit it commands and everything under it. A company commander has the same rights as the platoon and section leaders in their company, on every unit in it.

| Who | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Section Leader, Assistant Section Leader | Their section's rollcall, Section Practice marks, guests, fireteams and weekly report |
| Platoon Leader, Platoon Sergeant | All of that for every section in the platoon, the platoon's report and its training topic |
| Company command (Commander, XO, First Sergeant) | All of that for the company, its report and the company's fireteam rules |
| Battalion and regiment command | All of that for their battalion, or for the regiment |
| Acting lead | What the billet would give, for the unit an Admin assigned them |

## Staff and everyone else

| Who | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Staff grant holder | Read a unit an Admin gave them access to. Never change anything there. |
| Trooper with no command billet | Read the docs and their own account. The rest needs a billet or a grant. |

## Admins

Admins can see and change everything. Only they can edit the org structure, staff grants, rollcall templates and games, read the audit log, and assign acting leads.

## Who to ask

- **Wrong rank, billet or unit**: S1, since it comes from your milpac.
- **A button you should have and don't**: your chain of command, then S6.
- **Access for a staff role**: an Admin, for a staff grant.
