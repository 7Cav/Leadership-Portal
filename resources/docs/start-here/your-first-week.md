The **Start here** list on the right of every docs page tracks these. Tick each one off when you have done it.

## For everyone

1. [Sign in with your 7cav.us account](/docs/basics/sign-in).
2. [Read the roster](/docs/basics/read-the-roster) to find your unit and your troopers.
3. [Learn what others can do](/docs/start-here/what-others-can-do), so you know who to ask.

## If you lead a unit

Every week:

1. [Run this week's roll call](/docs/weekly-rollcall/run-rollcall): record the signatures, mark section practice and post it.
2. [Write and send your report](/docs/weekly-report/send-your-report) before Saturday 23:59 UTC.

Once, and then when it comes up:

1. [Link your troopers' in-game names](/docs/your-troopers/game-identities), so the game server's data finds them.
