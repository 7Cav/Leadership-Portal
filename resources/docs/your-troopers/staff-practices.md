> [!why] Staff are on no section's rollcall, so a practice they attend would be recorded nowhere. This keeps it on their own record, where their leaders can see it.

## Who this is for

1. Staff means troopers placed on an HQ (platoon, company, battalion or regiment staff) or in a staff department such as S6. Troopers in ELOA, the Reservists or New Recruits are not staff.
2. A staff member can record their own practices. Their staff leader (whoever commands the HQ or department they are placed in, and the chain above) and Admins can too.

## Add a practice

1. Open the staff member's milpac page. For your own, search for your name: everyone can open their own page.
2. In **Section practices attended**, choose **Add a practice**.
3. Pick the section whose practice they attended and the Cav week, then choose **Record practice**. Sections with nobody in them are not listed.

> [!good-to-know] One entry per section per week. If they were listed at that practice as a guest before they joined the staff, that entry stands and the portal says so. Only this week and the 11 before it can be entered, never a week before the portal's floor or a future week. Once the HQ the staff member was on that week has sent its weekly report for it, that week can no longer change here, even if they have moved since. A staff department has no weekly report, so its weeks stay open. Once a platoon or company HQ staff member has sent their own staff report for a week, that week can no longer change either.

## Read the list

1. Each entry names the week, the section and where it came from: **Self-reported by** the staff member, **Entered by** a leader or an Admin, or **Seen on the game server**.
2. A game server entry comes from a practice capture that saw them through their game identity. The capture reads the whole Cav week so far, so it means they were on the server that week.
3. The same list shows on the roster's trooper sheet, read only.

> [!good-to-know] A staff entry lists the staff member on the host section's rollcall, as a **Staff guest** in their own platoon or company and as a **Guest** elsewhere. It never counts in that section's numbers or reports, and gives no makeup. Platoon and company HQ staff say which practices they attended in their own staff report, which sets these entries for the week: see [Send your staff report](/docs/weekly-report/staff-report).

## Take one back off

1. Choose **Remove** next to an entry. The same people who can add one can remove it.
2. Entries seen on the game server cannot be removed by hand.
