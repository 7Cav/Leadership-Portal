> [!why] Staff are on no section's rollcall, so a practice they attend would be recorded nowhere. This keeps it on their own record, where their leaders can see it.

## Who this is for

1. Staff means troopers placed on an HQ (platoon, company, battalion or regiment staff) or in a staff department such as S6. Troopers in ELOA, the Reservists or New Recruits are not staff.
2. A staff member can record their own practices. Their staff leader (whoever commands the HQ or department they are placed in, and the chain above) and Admins can too.

## Add a practice

1. Open the staff member's milpac page.
2. In **Section practices attended**, choose **Add a practice**.
3. Pick the section whose practice they attended and the Cav week, then choose **Record practice**.

> [!good-to-know] One entry per section per week. Weeks before the portal's floor and future weeks are refused. Once the staff member's HQ has sent its weekly report for a week, that week can no longer change. A staff department has no weekly report, so its weeks stay open.

## Read the list

1. Each entry names the week, the section and where it came from: **Self-reported by** the staff member, **Entered by** a leader or an Admin, or **Seen on the game server**.
2. A game server entry comes from a practice capture that saw them through their game identity. The capture reads the whole Cav week so far, so it means they were on the server that week.
3. The same list shows on the roster's trooper sheet, read only.

> [!good-to-know] A staff entry never puts anyone on the host section's rollcall, never counts in its numbers or reports, and gives no makeup. The staff report does not show these yet.

## Take one back off

1. Choose **Remove** next to an entry. The same people who can add one can remove it.
2. Entries seen on the game server cannot be removed by hand.
