> [!why] Before you plan training or put someone forward for a role, the matrix shows at a glance who is qualified and who still needs a course.

## Open the matrix

1. Open a trooper's milpac page and choose their platoon's training readiness, or open it from the roster.
2. The matrix lists the unit's troopers down the side and the courses across the top.

## Read it

1. Each cell shows whether the trooper is qualified, in progress, or still needs the course.
2. The coverage line shows how much of the unit holds each course.

> [!good-to-know] Courses come from the Cav roster. If a course is missing, the trooper's milpac needs updating, not the portal.
