> [!why] The wiki gives a trooper the right to appeal an LOR to one level above the leader who issued it, and the appeal must be decided within 72 hours. The Appeals page puts those appeals in front of you with their clock.

## Who hears an appeal

1. An appeal goes to the commander of the unit one level above the unit the LOR was issued through: the Company Commander for an LOR from a platoon, the Battalion Commander for one from a company.
2. When a trooper appeals, you get a PM from their forum account, and **Appeals** appears in your sidebar with the number waiting for you.
3. Only you decide it, or an Admin acting as you, which the record names. The leader who issued it and the trooper cannot.

## Decide an appeal

1. Open **Appeals**. **Waiting** lists each appealed LOR with its issuer, the consequence and when your 72 hours run out; **Decided** keeps those decided or lapsed in the last 90 days.
2. Choose **Decide** to read the trooper's reason and the letter: **Open the full letter (PDF)** downloads it, **The record** opens its page.
3. Choose **Have not amended** to leave the letter as written, or **Have amended** to change the recommended consequence, the removal or the special punishments.
4. Give a **Reason**. The trooper, the issuer and S1 read it.
5. Choose **Record decision**. You may need to allow the portal to send PMs from your forum account first (see [Write a DCS or LOR](/docs/your-troopers/counseling)).

## After the decision

1. The decision is final. The LOR locks, and your rank, name and billet are recorded with the outcome on the form.
2. The trooper, the issuer and S1 get a PM from your forum account with the outcome and your reason.
3. When you amend the letter, the consequence as first written stays in the record's history.
4. An LOR does not lock while its appeal is waiting. An appeal not decided within 72 hours lapses: the letter stands as written and locks.
