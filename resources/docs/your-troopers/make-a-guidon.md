> [!why] A guidon is the swallowtail flag of a company and above, split on the diagonal into two colors. A saved guidon shows next to the unit on the roster, in the unit picker and on Org structure. It sits beside the wiki's own art and replaces nothing.

## Open the generator

1. Open the **Roster** on your company, battalion or the regiment.
2. Choose **Edit guidon** at the top. It shows for the unit's command team (CO, XO and 1SG of a company), the command above it, and Admins. Platoons and sections have no guidon.

## Pick the colors and the text

1. Pick a color for the top left and one for the bottom right, from the palette or with **Custom**.
2. The text starts as the battalion and the 7 on the top left (2/7) and the company letter on the bottom right (A). Change it if you need to: up to 6 letters, digits, /, - or spaces on each half.
3. The preview follows every change. The text is black or white, whichever reads better on its color.

> [!good-to-know] When the two colors are too close to tell apart, the generator says so. Pick colors with more contrast.

## Save or download it

1. Choose **Save guidon** to keep it. Nothing is saved until you do, and each save is recorded in the audit log.
2. Choose **Download SVG** or **Download PNG** for the wiki or the forum. The PNG is 300 pixels wide.
