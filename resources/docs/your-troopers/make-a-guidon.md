> [!why] A guidon is the flag of a company and above. A saved guidon shows next to the unit on the roster and on the company and platoon rows of its order of battle, in the unit picker, on Org structure, in the sidebar's unit switcher, on Home, roll call, the weekly reports, the trooper page and Staff reports. A platoon or section shows its company's guidon on its own pages. It sits beside the wiki's own art and replaces nothing.

## Open the generator

1. Open the **Roster** on your company, battalion or the regiment.
2. Choose **Edit guidon** at the top. It shows for the unit's command team (CO, XO and 1SG of a company), the command above it, and Admins. An Admin acting as one of them gets it too, and the save is recorded as the Admin acting for them. It never shows during view as. Platoons and sections have no guidon.

## Pick a format

Each format shows as a small preview with your colors and text. Switching keeps what you entered.

- **Diagonal**: two colors split corner to corner, the text on the top left and the bottom right.
- **Top and bottom**: the first color over the second, like the wiki's Regimental HQ and 3-7 HQ guidons. Place each text **Left** or **Center** on its half. The two texts always get the same letter height.
- **Quartered**: four quarters like a checkerboard, the first color top left and bottom right and the second top right and bottom left. The top text sits in the top left quarter and the bottom text in the bottom right one, **Left** or **Center**.
- **Solid**: one color.

## Pick the colors and the text

1. Pick a color for each half from the palette or with **Custom**.
2. The text starts as the battalion and the 7 on the top half (2/7) and the company letter on the bottom (A). Change it if you need to: up to 6 capital letters, digits, ., /, - or spaces on each half. Small letters turn into capitals as you type.
3. For a big number at the left, like the 7 on the wiki's Reserve guidon, fill in **Large character** with one or two letters or digits. It runs about two thirds of the height of the flag in the opposite color of each half, and the texts move to its right with a clear gap, in the opposite color too. They start on one left edge, or share a center line when both are a single character. On **Quartered** it fills the two left quarters and both texts move to the right quarters.
4. The text is set in a tall, narrow face like the regiment's official guidons.
5. The preview follows every change. Every text and the large character take the other color of the flag when it reads on their half, and black or white only when the two colors are too close for that (a solid guidon uses black or white).

> [!good-to-know] When the two colors are too close to tell apart, the generator says so. Pick colors with more contrast. A solid guidon has one color, so it has no warning.

## Save or download it

1. Choose **Save guidon** to keep it. Nothing is saved until you do, and each save is recorded in the audit log.
2. Choose **Download SVG** or **Download PNG** for the wiki or the forum. The PNG is 300 pixels wide. Both carry the text as shapes, so they look the same on any computer.

> [!good-to-know] Where guidons show small, like the unit lists, the roster's company and platoon rows, Org structure, Staff reports and next to a unit name in a page header, they show only their colors and split, without the text.
