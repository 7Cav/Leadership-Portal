> [!why] HQ staff owe one accountability each Cav week, and a trooper who misses their own section practice can make it up at another one. Each battalion, company, platoon and section can set what counts for that, so the staff report and the automatic makeups follow your unit's rules.

## The options

Every unit uses the same list. Tick any of them:

- **Section practice in own unit**
- **Section practice elsewhere in the battalion**
- **FTX**
- **Operation**
- **S7 class**
- **Other official event**
- **Public or training server hour with a leader present**
- **Section practice in another game**

On a battalion, company or platoon the options say what counts for its HQ staff report: the staff report offers only the ticked kinds, and its section list follows the section practice options. On a section they say what counts as a makeup for its troopers: an automatic makeup at another section's practice is given only when that practice is in the section's own company, elsewhere in its battalion, or in another game, as ticked. An option with nothing automatic behind it, such as the server hour, is kept and shown like the others, and is recorded by hand.

> [!good-to-know] A unit with nothing set uses the default for its echelon, which is the rule the portal always had: a battalion HQ counts a section practice in the battalion, an operation, a class or another official event. A company or platoon HQ counts a section practice in its own unit or an FTX. A section takes a makeup at any other section's practice. A unit under a battalion can never use an option the battalion has not ticked.

## Set a battalion

1. Admins and Regimental HQ staff open **Settings**, then **Org structure**, and select the battalion.
2. Under **What counts for Battalion HQ accountability**, tick the options and choose **Save**. **Use the default** puts the battalion back on the default.
3. Below it, greyed out, is what each of its companies, platoons and sections uses now, grouped by **Company HQ**, **Platoon HQ** and **Section makeups**, with (set) or (default). Each unit links to its roster's Admin tab, where it is changed.

## Set a company, platoon or section

1. Open the unit's **Roster** and choose the **Admin** tab. It shows only to those who may change something on it.
2. Under **What counts for Company HQ accountability** (or Platoon HQ, or **What counts as a makeup** on a section), tick the options and choose **Save**, or **Use the default**. The list offers only what the battalion allows.
3. On a company or platoon, the **Platoon HQ** and **Sections** cards below list the units under it. Open one to set it the same way. A unit with nobody in it or under it is left out, as in the order of battle; it comes back once someone is placed in it.

The battalion's HQ staff (those placed on the battalion itself), Admins and Regimental HQ staff can change the units under a battalion. The battalion's own set is changed only on Org structure. Every change is in the audit log. View as never changes anything. Act as can, as the person acted as.

> [!good-to-know] When an Admin or Regimental HQ staff changes a unit under a battalion, the battalion's HQ staff get a notification in the bell at the top of the page, with a link to the unit's Admin tab. Nobody is told when the battalion's own HQ staff make the change. Choose **Mark all read** to clear the count. A notification is deleted 14 days after you read it. The bell is hidden during view as and act as.
