> [!why] The roster is refreshed from the Cav roster on a schedule. When a trooper joins or moves today, a sync puts them on your rollcall without waiting for the next one.

## Start a sync

1. Choose **Roster** in the sidebar and open the unit.
2. Choose **Sync this unit**.
3. It runs in the background. Reload the page after a minute.

## The cooldown

1. After a sync, the unit and the units above and below it cannot be synced again for 5 minutes.
2. Try sooner and the portal asks you to wait. One sync is enough: it fetches the whole unit.

> [!good-to-know] If a trooper is still missing after a sync, their milpac on 7cav.us has not been updated yet. Ask S1.
