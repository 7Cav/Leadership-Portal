> [!why] The milpac page puts a trooper's rank, billet, courses, service record and transfers in one place, so you can check them before a rollcall or a counseling without leaving the portal.

## Open the search

1. Choose **Find trooper** in the top bar, or press the `/` key on any page.

## Type part of their name

1. Type at least two letters of their username. A rank in front and a space for the dot both work: "SGT Vasquez R".
2. To find someone by milpac ID, type the number, such as `#1234`.
3. Use the arrow keys or the mouse to pick them. Their milpac page opens.

> [!good-to-know] You only find troopers in the units you lead or have been given access to. Someone outside them does not show up, and the search says so.

## Read the milpac page

1. The top block shows rank, billet, MOS, time in service and grade, and section.
2. Below it are their courses, their service record and their transfers between units, newest first.
3. The quick links open their section's roster and rollcall, and their platoon's training readiness.
4. The **Counseling** panel holds the trooper's DCSs and LORs that you may see.
5. On a staff member's page, **Section practices attended** lists the practices they went to. See [Record a practice a staff member attended](/docs/your-troopers/staff-practices).

> [!good-to-know] Awards are not synced from the Cav roster yet, and the page says so. Use the Cav milpac for awards.
