---
title: Preview with View as
summary: See the portal as a unit's leader sees it, without changing anything.
section: basics
order: 4
audience: everyone
---

> [!why] View as answers "what does my section leader see?" before you ask them to do something. It only narrows what you see, and nothing can be changed while it is on.

## Start a preview

1. Choose **View as** in the top bar.
2. Pick a unit you lead, or a unit under it. Admins can also pick a person. A unit with no troopers in it or under it is not listed.
3. A gold bar at the top says who you are viewing as.
4. You stay on the same page, for the unit you picked: from a battalion's roster you land on the roster of the section you picked. Any other page stays open. If the unit or person you picked cannot see it, the page says so; the gold bar stays.

## Look around, then exit

1. Every page now shows only what that unit's leader would see. The roster and the roll call open where that leader would land.
2. The gold bar says the preview is read only. Buttons that would change something are greyed out or refused while it is on.
3. Choose **Exit view as** on the gold bar to leave. You stay on the page you were on.

## Move between your units without a preview

1. Open the block at the top of the sidebar. It names the top unit you can see.
2. Choose a unit. The page you are on opens for that unit, or its roster if the page is not about a unit.

> [!good-to-know] The unit list only moves you around. It does not change what you can see or do; View as does that.

> [!good-to-know] Starting and ending a preview is recorded in the audit log.

> [!good-to-know] View as only lets you look. To work as someone, use **Act as** in the user menu: a leader can act as a billet holder below them, or as someone junior to them in their own command team, such as a Section Leader as their ASL or a Commander as their XO. Nobody can act as someone above them or beside them.
