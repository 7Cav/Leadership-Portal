> [!why] View as answers "what does my section leader see?" before you ask them to do something. It only narrows what you see, and nothing can be changed while it is on.

## Start a preview

1. Choose **View as** in the top bar.
2. Pick a unit you lead, or a unit under it. Admins can also pick a person.
3. A gold bar at the top says who you are viewing as.

## Look around

1. Every page now shows only what that unit's leader would see.
2. Buttons that would change something are refused while the preview is on.

## Leave the preview

1. Choose **Exit view as** on the gold bar.

> [!good-to-know] Starting and ending a preview is recorded in the audit log.
