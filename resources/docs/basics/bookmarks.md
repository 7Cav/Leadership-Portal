> [!why] A bookmark opens a page you check often, like another section's roster or your platoon's report, in one click from any page.

## Add a bookmark

1. Open the page: a unit's roster, report, roll call or training readiness, a trooper's milpac page, **Staff reports**, a guide in the docs, or a Settings page you can open.
2. Choose the bookmark button beside the page name in the top bar. It fills in, and the page shows under **Bookmarks** in the sidebar.
3. To remove it, choose the button again.

> [!good-to-know] A unit's report bookmark opens the current week's report every time, not the week you were on when you added it.

## Keep your list in order

1. You have 10 bookmark slots. When all 10 are in use, the button says so, and you remove one to add another.
2. In the sidebar, choose the three dots beside a bookmark and choose **Move up**, **Move down** or **Remove**.

## When a bookmark goes away

1. A bookmark is removed on its own when you can no longer open its page: the unit leaves your units or is deleted, the trooper is no longer in your units, or you lose access to a Settings page.
2. Your bookmarks are yours alone. They are hidden while you view as or act as someone, and in the tutorial.
