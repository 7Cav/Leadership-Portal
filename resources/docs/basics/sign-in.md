> [!why] The portal knows who you are from your forum account and your milpac. Your billet on the Cav roster decides what you can see and change, so nobody has to set up your access by hand.

## Open the portal

1. Go to the portal's address.
2. Choose **Continue with 7Cav**.

## Approve on the forum

1. Sign in to 7cav.us if you are not already.
2. Approve the portal when the forum asks. You come back to the portal's Home page.

> [!good-to-know] Your forum account must be linked to a serving milpac. If the portal says it isn't, ask S1 to check your milpac, or S6 if your milpac is fine.

## Sign out

1. Open your name at the bottom of the sidebar.
2. Choose **Sign out**.
