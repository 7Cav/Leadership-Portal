> [!why] The tutorial is the portal itself with a made-up unit in it. You can click everything, send everything and make mistakes, and none of it reaches real troopers, the forum, Discord, the staff Sheet or the audit log.

## Start the tutorial

1. Open **Docs** in the sidebar.
2. Under Getting started tutorial, choose **Start the tutorial**.
3. The portal gets a blue frame and a blue bar at the top. That is how you know you are in the tutorial.

## Follow the steps

1. A card at the bottom right says what to do on this step, with a short checklist.
2. The checklist ticks itself as you do each thing on the page.
3. **Next step** opens once every item on the checklist is ticked. Choose **Skip** to move on without them, or **Back** to return to a step. The dots in the blue bar show where you are.
4. The steps follow your billet. A Section Leader runs roll call and sends the section report. HQ staff also send a staff report. Everyone reads the roster, and Admins also look at the Admin pages.

## The practice unit

1. Your practice unit is 9-7, a made-up battalion with Alpha Company, 1st Platoon and two sections.
2. You are in it at your own level: a Section Leader leads 1st Section, a Platoon Leader leads 1st Platoon.
3. Every other trooper is made up, apart from two of the people who build the portal.
4. The practice data is yours alone. Nobody else sees what you do in it.

> [!good-to-know] The practice troopers are made up, so **View on 7cav.us** on their milpac opens the milpac of one of the portal's maintainers instead. When the last step is done, the card says **Finished**.

> [!good-to-know] When you finish a step, the same step of the Getting started tutorial is ticked too.

## Start over or leave

1. Choose **Start over** in the blue bar to get fresh practice data and begin again.
2. Choose **Exit tutorial** to go back to the guides home. Your practice data is removed.
3. View as and Act as are not available in the tutorial. Stop them before you start it.
