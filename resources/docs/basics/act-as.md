> [!why] When a leader below you is away, act as lets you do what they would do, such as run their rollcall or send their report, without asking an Admin. Unlike View as, everything you change is real.

## Who you can act as

1. A leader can act as a serving billet holder below them in their own units: a Platoon Leader as their Section Leaders, a Company Commander as anyone holding a billet in the company's platoons and sections.
2. Inside your own unit's command team, you can also act as everyone junior to you: a Company Commander as their XO and First Sergeant, a Platoon Leader as their Platoon Sergeant, a Section Leader as their Assistant Section Leader. At the regiment and in a battalion the order is CO, XO, then the Command Sergeant Major or Sergeant Major. Never the other way round.
3. A staff grant gives no act as, because it only reads.
4. An Admin can act as anyone who could sign in, or as the leader of any unit, even one whose billet nobody holds.

## Start acting as someone

1. Open the menu under your name at the foot of the sidebar and choose **Act as**. The menu only offers it when there is someone below you.
2. Find the person in **Billet holders below you** and choose them.
3. Read what it means and choose **Act as them**. Home opens as they see it.

> [!good-to-know] Admins start from View as: preview a person or a unit, then choose **Act as them**, **Act as this billet** or, for someone who has never signed in, **Act as their billet** on the gold bar.

## Work while acting

1. A red bar at the top says **Acting as** and who. It stays on screen on every page.
2. You have exactly their permissions and lose your own until you stop. Every page shows what they would see, and every button does what it would do for them.
3. Everything you change is recorded with both names: a report sent while acting shows as sent by "Taylor.C for Jay.JH". When an Admin acts as a billet, it shows as "Taylor.C acting as Section Leader 1/1/A/1-7". The audit log names you as the one who did it, acting as them.

> [!good-to-know] Starting and stopping act as is recorded in the audit log.

## Stop acting

1. Choose **Stop acting** on the red bar. You are yourself again and Home opens.
2. If the person stops qualifying while you act, for example they leave their billet or the Cav, act as ends on its own and you are yourself again.

## What act as cannot do

1. You cannot act as yourself, anyone above you, a peer outside your command team order, or an Admin.
2. You cannot act as someone whose own command covers you. If you are acting lead of the platoon above your own section, you cannot act as your own Section Leader.
3. You cannot act as someone whose own access, such as a staff grant or an acting lead, reaches outside your units.
4. No chains: while you act as someone, you cannot act as anyone else. Stop first.
5. An Admin who acts as another Admin gets none of their Admin rights, only their billet, acting leads and grants. An Admin acting as a leader can act only as the people below that leader; to be an Admin again, stop acting.
6. Acting as someone never lets you file counseling on yourself or see your own counseling records.

## Act as compared with View as

1. [View as](/docs/basics/view-as) is a read-only preview with a gold bar. Nothing can be changed while it is on.
2. Act as has a red bar, and every change is real. Use View as to check what someone sees, and act as only when something has to be done in their place.
