## Weeks and attendance

- Cav week: Saturday 0000Z to Friday 2359Z. Every roll call, report and number in the portal is counted per Cav week. The portal names a week by its last day, as the Google Form does, followed by both its dates: Week Ending 30OCT26 (24OCT26 to 30OCT26). In tables and lists it shows the dates alone: 24OCT26 to 30OCT26.
- Roll call: the forum thread troopers sign each week to say whether they will attend, and the portal's screen where a leader records it.
- Section practice (SP): the weekly practice a section holds. Each trooper is marked Attended, Not attended, Excused or Make-up complete.
- Excused: a week that counts for nothing, like LOA. A leader sets it, or it is set automatically for a trooper's first week after a transfer into a section or after joining.
- Make-up requested: a roll call answer that counts as signed. The trooper owes a make-up for that week until one is recorded.
- Make-up complete: a missed practice made up, for example at another section's practice instead of your own. It counts for your own section.
- LOA: leave of absence. A trooper whose leave covers the week is locked on the roll call and posts as LOA.
- Week floor: how far back a roll call or report can still be changed. Older weeks are read only.

## Units and people

- ORBAT: order of battle, the units and who holds each billet.
- Milpac: a trooper's military personnel record on 7cav.us, and their page in the portal.
- Echelon: a level of the chain of command: regiment, battalion, company, platoon, section.
- Section: the smallest line unit, led by a Section Leader and an Assistant Section Leader.
- Platoon: two or more sections, led by a Platoon Leader and a Platoon Sergeant.
- Headcount: everyone serving placed in a unit or under it, HQ staff and staff departments included. New Recruits, ELOA and Reservists count only on their own group. Home and the weekly report show it beside In roll call, the troopers in sections that the rates are counted over. The roster and Org structure label it Headcount too. It never changes that number or any rate.
- Billet: a position on the Cav roster, such as Section Leader 1/1/A/1-7. Your billet decides what you can see and change.
- Acting lead: a trooper an Admin has put in charge of a unit without the billet.
- View as: a read-only preview of the portal as a unit's leader, or for an Admin as a person.
- Act as: working in a billet holder's place with exactly their permissions. Changes are real and recorded with your name and theirs.

## Reports and games

- Training plan: a platoon's training subjects for each Cav week of a month, approved by its company.
- Weekly report: a unit's numbers, training subject and notes for the week, sent up the chain. Sending freezes it.
- Roll-up: a platoon's or higher unit's report, added up from the sent reports below it.
- Staff report: a platoon or company HQ staff member's own weekly report of the section practices they attended.
- Staff guest: a staff member listed on a roll call of a section in their own platoon or company. Shown only, never counted.
- Game identity: a trooper's in-game name and ID, one per game, so the server data can be matched to them.
- Unit game: the game a unit plays, set by an Admin on Org structure. Every unit under it plays the same game unless it has its own.
- Unmatched player: someone the game server saw at a practice who is not linked to any trooper yet.
- JSAM: Joint Service Achievement Medal. A guest who keeps attending another unit's practices may be a candidate.

## Counseling

- DCS: Developmental Counseling Statement, a non-punitive written counseling of a trooper, filed with its signed document.
- LOR: Letter of Reprimand, a punitive written reprimand, filed with its signed document.
- No favorable action: the 30 days after an LOR. The counseling panel shows the date it ends.
