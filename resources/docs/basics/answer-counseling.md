> [!why] The counseling form has a part for you: your plan of action and your comments. On a Letter of Reprimand you also say whether you appeal it. Answering in the portal puts your part on the official form.

## Find it

1. When your leader shares a record with you, you get their After-Action PM on the forum with a link to it.
2. It is also on your **Home** with a countdown, and on your own milpac page in the **Counseling** panel.

## Add your part

1. Open the record. **What your leader wrote** shows the infraction, the regulation, the notes of the discussion, their plan of action and, on an LOR, the recommended consequence.
2. Under **Your part**, write your plan of action and any comments. Both are optional.
3. Choose **Submit response**. You submit once; after that the record shows it as acknowledged.

## Appeal an LOR

1. On an LOR, choose **I do not wish to appeal** or **I wish to appeal**. The commander who would hear your appeal is named there.
2. To appeal, give your reason. The portal sends it by PM from your own forum account, so it may first ask you to allow PMs from your forum account.
3. Answer within 24 hours of the After-Action PM. Without an answer the letter stands as written.
4. The appeal is decided within 72 hours, and the decision is final. You get it by PM.

## How long you see it

1. You see a DCS for 30 days after it was shared with you.
2. You see an LOR permanently.
3. Download either with **Download PDF**: the portal fills in the regiment's official form.
