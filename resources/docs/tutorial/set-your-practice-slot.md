With a practice slot, the portal reads the game server within ten minutes of your practice ending and marks who was there. Without one, you mark everyone by hand and make-ups are not given from the game server.

## Set the slot

1. On **Roll call**, choose the pencil next to the practice time, or next to **No practice slot**.
2. Pick the weekday and the start time in Zulu, then choose **Save slot**.

A section practice is always one hour. A trooper counts as attended after 40 minutes on the server during that hour, unless your battalion set another minimum.

## Move one week only

1. Choose **This week only**.
2. Pick another day and time in the same Cav week and choose **Move this week**, or choose **Cancel this week**.
3. **Back to the usual time** undoes either.

> [!good-to-know] A week the game server already read, or whose report was sent, keeps its time.
