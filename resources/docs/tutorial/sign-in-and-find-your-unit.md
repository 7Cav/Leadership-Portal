You need your 7cav.us forum account and a serving milpac. Nobody has to set up your access: your billet on the Cav roster decides what you can see and change.

## Sign in

1. Open the portal and choose **Continue with 7Cav**.
2. Sign in to 7cav.us if the forum asks, and approve the portal.
3. You land on **Home**.

## Find your unit on Home

1. The unit picker at the top of the sidebar shows the unit you lead.
2. The week bar shows the days of the Cav week and when the report is due. A week reads like Week Ending 30OCT26 (24OCT26 to 30OCT26).
3. **Needs attention** lists what is holding up your unit this week, each with a link to fix it.

> [!good-to-know] Times in the portal are in Zulu, such as 2359Z, with your own time beside them where it helps.
