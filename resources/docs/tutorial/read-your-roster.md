The roster is the portal's copy of the Cav roster for the units you lead. A scheduled sync keeps it up to date, and the page says when it last ran.

## Open your roster

1. Choose **Roster** in the sidebar. It opens on the unit you lead.
2. **Order of battle** lists your troopers by billet.
3. **Attendance matrix** shows each trooper's section practice mark for the last 20 Cav weeks.

## Look for warnings

1. A warning icon next to a section means it has no practice slot yet. You set one in step 4.
2. A warning icon next to a name means the trooper has no identity for your unit's game. You fix that in step 5.

> [!good-to-know] Choose a trooper to open their details on the side: their section, their leader and 20 weeks of marks, with where each mark came from.
