That is the whole weekly loop: roll call, then the report. These are the places to go when something is unclear.

> [!good-to-know] For any technical issue, question or concern about the portal, open a ticket with S6.

## Help on any page

1. Choose **Help** in the top bar. It opens the guide for the page you are on.
2. On any docs page, press `/` or Ctrl K to search the guides.

## Ask a person

1. [What others can do](/docs/start-here/what-others-can-do) says who can change what, so you know who to ask.
2. A problem with your milpac goes to S1.
3. Something not working in the portal, a question or a concern: open a ticket with S6.
