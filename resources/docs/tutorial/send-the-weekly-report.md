Your report is what your chain of command reads about your unit's week. It is due by 2359Z on the Saturday after the Cav week ends: for Week Ending 30OCT26 (24OCT26 to 30OCT26), that is 31OCT26 2359Z.

## Check the numbers

1. Choose **Reports** in the sidebar. A section leader lands on their own section's report.
2. The numbers come from your roll call. If one is wrong, choose **Open roll call**, fix the mark and come back.

## Work through Ready to send

1. **Ready to send** lists what is still open.
2. Under **Unaccounted troopers**, pick a reason for each one, or choose **Keep suggested reason**.
3. Fill in the **Training subject** and your **Notes to send with this report**. The draft saves as you type.

## Freeze and send

1. Tick **I have read the preview and my notes as command will see them**.
2. Choose **Freeze and send**, give a reason for the record and confirm.

> [!warning] Sending freezes the numbers, and that week's roll call is locked. Until the report is due you can still choose **Edit report**, change it and send it again. After that, what your chain of command reads never changes.
