Roll call is the weekly task the rest of the portal is built on. Your section's report comes from it, so finish it every week.

## Record the signatures

1. Choose **Roll call** in the sidebar. It opens on your section and this week.
2. In step 1, **Signatures**, set each trooper as they signed the forum thread: **Will attend**, **Will not attend**, **Make-up requested**, **Excused** or **Unsigned**.
3. Every change saves as you click.

Troopers on leave are already locked under **On LOA**. You do not mark them.

## Mark section practice

1. In step 2, **Section practice**, check the marks. With a practice slot set, the portal reads the game server by itself after practice and fills them in.
2. Fix any mark that is wrong. Once the practice is over, choose **Confirm held**, or **Not held** if it did not take place.
3. A trooper who trained at another section's practice is **Make-up complete**. With a practice slot set, the game server records that make-up for you.

## Review and post

1. In step 3, **Review and post**, pick your **Template** and choose **Copy BBCode**.
2. Paste it as a reply in the forum's roll call thread. The portal never posts to the forum for you.
3. Back in the portal, choose **Mark roll call posted**.

> [!warning] If you change a mark after posting, the page says the copied BBCode is out of date. Repost it, then choose **Mark posted again**.
