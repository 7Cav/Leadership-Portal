Roll call is the weekly task the rest of the portal is built on. Your section's report comes from it, so finish it every week. The steps are along the top: **Create**, **Post**, **Sign and mark**, **Close for review**.

## Create the roll call

1. Choose **Roll call** in the sidebar. It opens on your section and this week, on step 1, **Create**.
2. Check the **Training date**. It comes from your section's practice slot; changing it moves this week's practice only.
3. Check the **Training topic**. It comes from your platoon's topic, and is locked when your platoon enforces it.
4. Choose **Create roll call**.

## Post it

1. In step 2, **Post**, pick your **Template** and choose **Copy BBCode**.
2. Paste it as a reply in the forum's roll call thread. The portal never posts to the forum for you.
3. Paste the thread's link under **Forum thread link** if you like, then choose **Mark as posted, continue**.

## Sign and mark

1. In step 3, **Sign and mark**, set each trooper's signature as they signed the thread: **Will attend**, **Will not attend**, **Make-up requested**, **Excused** or **Unsigned**. Every change saves as you click.
2. Below the signatures, check the practice marks. With a practice slot set, the portal reads the game server by itself after practice and fills them in. Fix any mark that is wrong.
3. Once the practice is over, choose **Confirm practice took place**, or **Practice did not take place** if it did not take place.

Troopers on leave are already locked under **On LOA**. You do not mark them.

## Close for review

1. In step 4, **Close for review**, choose **Copy updated BBCode**, edit your forum post with it, then tick **I edited the forum post**.
2. Write your notes, and give a reason for anyone who missed practice.
3. Choose **Close and send for review**. The weekly report opens to send; the next tutorial step walks it.

> [!warning] If you change a mark or the topic after posting, the page says the copied BBCode is out of date. Copy it again and edit your forum post.
