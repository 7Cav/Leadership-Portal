> [!why] HQ staff attend at least one section practice a week. The overview shows in one place who did, for every HQ whose staff you read.

## Open it

1. Choose **Staff reports** in the sidebar. It opens the week that just ended, the one whose reports are due.
2. Use the arrows beside the week to go back a week or forward again.
3. If you send a staff report yourself, it sits at the top. Choose **Send your staff report** while it is not sent yet.

## Read the list

1. Staff are grouped by their HQ, from the top of the tree down. Each line shows the name, billet, status, what they attended and whether they met the one practice a week.
2. **Sent** means they sent it, **Not sent yet** means the week has ended and it is still missing, and **Not due yet** means the week is still running.
3. **Open report** opens their full staff report.

> [!good-to-know] You see the staff of every platoon or company HQ under a unit you command or read: a company commander sees their platoon HQs, battalion and regiment command see company and platoon HQs, and Admins see all. A PL or PSG sees only their own report. For a week that has ended, each HQ lists who was on it when the week ended.
