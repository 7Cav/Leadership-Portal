> [!why] Your report is what your chain of command reads about your unit's week. Sending it freezes the numbers, so what they read never changes after you send.

## Open your report

1. Choose **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. A section leader lands on their own section's report. Above a section, you see your units for the week; choose the one to open.
3. The report is due by Saturday 23:59 UTC, the end of the Saturday after the Cav week.

## Check the numbers

1. The numbers are counted from your rollcall and Section Practice marks for the week.
2. If one is wrong, choose **Open rollcall**, fix the mark, and come back. The report updates.

## Work through Ready to send

1. **Ready to send** lists what is still open: rollcall complete, practice marked, unaccounted troopers explained, training subject filled, notes reviewed.
2. Under **Unaccounted troopers**, pick a reason for each one.
3. Fill in the **Training subject**. Last week's subject is offered; choose **Use it again** to keep it.
4. Write your **Notes to send with this report**.

> [!good-to-know] The draft saves as you type. You can leave and come back to it any time before you send.

## Freeze and send

1. Tick **I have read the preview and my notes as command will see them**.
2. Choose **Freeze & send**, give a reason for the record, and confirm.
3. If something is still open and the report must go out anyway, choose **Send anyway**. What was open is recorded with it.

> [!good-to-know] A platoon's or higher unit's report adds up the sent reports of the units below it. A unit that has not sent yet is left out of the numbers and named, so send yours on time.
