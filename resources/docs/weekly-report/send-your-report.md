> [!why] Your report is what your chain of command reads about your unit's week. Sending it freezes the numbers, so what they read never changes after you send.

## Open your report

1. Choose **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. A section leader lands on their own section's report. Above a section, you see your units for the week; choose the one to open.
3. The report is due by Saturday 2359Z, the end of the Saturday after the Cav week.

## Check the numbers

1. The numbers are counted from your roll call and section practice marks for the week.
2. If one is wrong, choose **Open roll call**, fix the mark, and come back. The report updates.

## Work through Ready to send

1. **Ready to send** lists what is still open: roll call complete, practice marked, practice confirmed, unaccounted troopers explained, training subject filled, notes reviewed.
2. Under **Unaccounted troopers**, pick a reason for each one. When the portal knows why, such as a trooper who moved out of your section this week, left the Cav, or posted leave the sync could not read, that reason is already chosen and the hint says why. Choose **Keep suggested reason**, or pick another. A suggestion you have not kept is not saved or sent.
3. Fill in the **Training subject**. Last week's subject is offered; choose **Use it again** to keep it. It is the same topic as the roll call's **Training topic**, so one set on the roll call is already here, as are notes and reasons written on its close step. A section's report opens with a summary of its roll call: the training, the topic, the forum thread, and how the week signed and attended.
4. Write your **Notes to send with this report**.

> [!good-to-know] If your unit's game can never be read automatically, such as Battlefield 6, the report says so once near the top in orange: all attendance is self-reported. Every mark is then entered by hand, and nothing on the report asks for game server data.

> [!good-to-know] The draft saves as you type. You can leave and come back to it any time before you send.

## Freeze and send

1. Tick **I have read the preview and my notes as command will see them**.
2. Choose **Freeze and send**, give a reason for the record, and confirm.
3. If something is still open and the report must go out anyway, choose **Send anyway**. What was open is recorded with it.

> [!good-to-know] The practice must be confirmed before the report can be sent, even with **Send anyway**. On the roll call, choose **Confirm practice took place** or **Practice did not take place** once the practice is over. A section with nobody on its roll call has nothing to confirm.

> [!good-to-know] If a trooper asked for a make-up this week and none is recorded, the dialog lists them under **Make-ups still owed**. Answer **Made it up** or **Did not** for each before you send. **Made it up** marks them **Make-up complete** on the roll call; **Did not** leaves their mark as it is. Your answers are kept with the report.

> [!good-to-know] A platoon's or higher unit's report adds up the sent reports of the units below it. A unit that has not sent yet is left out of the numbers and named, so send yours on time. A unit with nobody in any section under it, such as a platoon whose only troopers are its leaders, has no report to send, and the unit above it does not wait on it.

> [!good-to-know] The report page opens on the current Cav week. To send a late report, use the arrows beside the week to go back to the week it is for first. While last week's report is not sent, the page says so and links to it.

## Edit a sent report

Until the report is due, 2359Z on the Saturday after the Cav week, a sent report can still be changed. Anyone who could send it can edit it, not only whoever sent it. After that, ask a leader above you to unlock it (see below).

1. On the sent report, choose **Edit report**, give a reason for the record, and confirm. That week's roll call opens again, so you can fix marks too.
2. Change what you need. Edits are not saved as a draft: they stay on the page until you send.
3. Choose **Send again**, give a reason, and confirm. The new numbers and notes replace the sent ones. The version you replaced is kept, and the audit log records both reasons.
4. The **History** tab lists the versions you replaced under **Earlier versions**. Open one to see its numbers and notes, who reopened it and who sent it again, when and why.

> [!good-to-know] Until you send again, the version you sent stays the report your chain of command reads. If you never send again, it locks at the deadline as it was.

## Request an edit of a locked report

Once a sent report is past its deadline, it is locked. To change it, ask a leader above you to unlock it.

1. On the sent report, choose **Request edit**, say what needs to change and why, and choose **Send request**. The leaders one level above your unit get it in their bell. If that unit has no leader with a portal account, the next unit up gets it.
2. A leader above you opens the report from the bell and chooses **Approve** or **Deny**, with a note if they want. Any leader higher up, or an Admin, can decide it from the report too. You get their answer in your bell.
3. Once approved, the report is unlocked until the coming Saturday 2359Z. Choose **Edit report** and send it again as above. After that time it locks again.

> [!good-to-know] If a report went into the wrong week, request an edit of that week's report and send the right one once it is approved. Then send the late report from its own week.

> [!good-to-know] If a unit below yours changes its report after you sent yours, your sent report names it. Your numbers do not change on their own: edit your report and send it again to include the change.
