> [!why] When the whole platoon trains on one subject, setting it once saves every section leader typing it, and keeps the reports consistent.

## Who can set it

1. The platoon's chain of command: the Platoon Leader and Platoon Sergeant, and anyone above them in the company.
2. Section leaders see the topic on their report but cannot set it.

## Set the topic

1. Open your platoon's report for the week.
2. Under **Training topic for your sections**, type the subject.
3. To make it a starting point that sections can change, leave **Enforce it, so sections cannot change it** unticked. To lock it on every section's report, tick it.
4. Choose **Apply to your sections**.

## Change or clear it

1. Edit the subject and apply it again, or choose **Clear**.
2. A section whose report is already sent keeps the subject it was sent with.
