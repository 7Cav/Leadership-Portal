> [!why] A training plan sets the platoon's subjects for a whole month at once and puts them in front of the company before the weeks start. Once approved, nobody has to type the subject week by week.

## Who does what

1. The platoon's chain of command writes the plan: the Platoon Leader and Platoon Sergeant, anyone above them in the company, and Admins.
2. The company's command reviews it: the company, the battalion and regiment above it, and Admins. A Platoon Leader cannot approve their own plan unless they also command the company.
3. Everyone else who can see the platoon, a staff grant included, reads the plan but cannot change it.

## Open the plan

1. Open your platoon's report and choose **Training plan**. The plan opens on the month of the week you were on.
2. Use the arrows next to the month to move between months. A plan can be written from the week floor up to a year ahead.
3. Above a platoon, the same page lists every platoon's plan for the month, with where it stands and how many weeks are planned. Choose **Open** to read one.

## Fill in the weeks

1. Under **Training subject for each week**, the month holds every Cav week whose Saturday falls in that month, so a month has 4 or 5 weeks. Each week is labelled by its Saturday and Friday (**28NOV26 to 04DEC26**), so the last week of a month can show a date in the next month.
2. Type a subject for each week you want to plan. A week left blank is not planned, and its sections fill in their own subject as usual. At least one week needs a subject.
3. To lock the subjects on every section's report, tick **Lock these subjects, so sections cannot change them**. Leave it unticked to give sections a starting point they can change.

## Send it for approval

1. Choose **Send for approval**. The plan shows **Waiting for approval**, and the line above the button says what happens next.
2. Every save sends the plan to the company again. Changing a plan that is already approved needs approval again; until then, the subjects from the last approval stay on the reports.

## Review a plan

1. Open the platoon's plan. When it is waiting for approval and you command the company, a panel headed **Review for** and the company's name shows beside it.
2. Choose **Approve** to apply the plan.
3. To send it back instead, write what should change under **What should change** and choose **Send back**. The plan shows **Sent back**, and the platoon sees your comment above its weeks.
4. A platoon whose plan was sent back changes it and chooses **Send for approval** again. It can also send it again unchanged.

> [!good-to-know] If the platoon changed the plan after you opened it, the approval is refused. Reload the page and review it again.

## What approval does

1. Each planned week's subject becomes that week's platoon training topic, locked or as a default as the plan says. Every section's report for that week starts with it. See [Set the platoon training topic](/docs/weekly-report/platoon-training-topic).
2. From then on, a planned week's topic can only change through the plan. On the platoon's report, the topic field says it comes from the approved training plan, and it cannot be set, unlocked or cleared there.
3. When you approve a revision that leaves a week blank, the topic an earlier approval of the same plan wrote for that week is cleared. A topic someone set by hand stays.

> [!good-to-know] A week whose platoon report is already sent, or that is before the week floor, keeps its subject. The plan cannot change it, and approval leaves it as it is. A section report that is already sent keeps the subject it was sent with.
