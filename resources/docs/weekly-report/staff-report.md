> [!why] HQ staff attend at least one section practice a week, preferably in their own unit. Your staff report is how your chain of command knows you did.

## Who sends one

1. Every trooper placed on a platoon, company or battalion HQ: the PL, PSG, CO, XO, 1SG, the battalion command team and anyone else placed there, ACD included. Regiment HQ, staff departments, ELOA, the Reservists and New Recruits send none.
2. You send your own. Nobody can send it for you, not even an Admin.

## Open it

1. Choose **Staff report** in the sidebar, or **Open staff report** on Home. It opens the week that just ended, the one whose report is due.
2. You send it once the Cav week is over, from Saturday 00:00 UTC. It is due when the unit reports are: Saturday 23:59 UTC, the end of the Saturday after the Cav week.
3. The running week opens read-only and says from when you can send it.

## Answer and pick the practices

1. Answer **Did you attend a section practice this week?** with Yes or No.
2. For Yes, pick the type: **Section practice (SP)** or **FTX**. SP is picked to start with.
3. For an SP, tick each practice you attended. Your own unit's sections are listed first, and **Find a section** narrows the list.
4. For an FTX, give its name and its date, a day in that Cav week. Ticking the sections that took part is optional.
5. Practices already entered for you this week are ticked, with who entered them. Untick one you did not attend: sending takes it off.
6. Add any other events you attended, and notes, if you like. Both are optional.

> [!good-to-know] **Seen on the game server** means a capture saw you on the server that Cav week. It is never ticked for you, because the capture reads the whole week, not one practice.

## Send it

1. Choose **Send staff report**. Once sent, it cannot be changed.
2. Each practice you picked lists you on that section's roll call: as a **Staff guest** in your own platoon, company or battalion, and as a **Guest** anywhere else. You never count in that section's numbers.
3. Your report is also copied to the regiment's reports sheet. Your report page says **Sent to the sheet** once it is there.

> [!good-to-know] Your unit's report shows whether its HQ staff sent theirs, for the leaders above it. Sending your HQ's own report does not stop you sending yours. For a week that has ended, the portal goes by the HQ you were on when it ended, so you still send it after a move.

## Read your staff's reports

1. If you command or read a unit above a platoon, company or battalion HQ, the sidebar shows **Staff reports** instead of **Staff report**. It lists every HQ staff member whose report you read, for one week, with your own on top.
2. A PL or PSG reads only their own report and keeps **Staff report**. See [Read your staff's reports](/docs/weekly-report/staff-reports) for the overview.
