> [!why] Platoon and company HQ staff attend at least one section practice a week, preferably in their own platoon or company. Your staff report is how your chain of command knows you did.

## Who sends one

1. Every trooper placed on a platoon or company HQ: the PL, PSG, CO, XO, 1SG and anyone else placed there. Battalion and regiment HQ, staff departments, ELOA, the Reservists and New Recruits send none.
2. You send your own. Nobody can send it for you, not even an Admin.

## Open it

1. Choose **Staff report** in the sidebar, or **Open staff report** on Home. It opens the week that just ended, the one whose report is due.
2. You send it once the Cav week is over, from Saturday 00:00 UTC. It is due when the unit reports are: Saturday 23:59 UTC, the end of the Saturday after the Cav week.
3. The running week opens read-only and says from when you can send it.

## Answer and pick the practices

1. Answer **Did you attend a section practice this week?** with Yes or No.
2. For Yes, tick each practice you attended. Your own platoon's or company's sections are listed first, and **Find a section** narrows the list.
3. Practices already entered for you this week are ticked, with who entered them. Untick one you did not attend: sending takes it off.

> [!good-to-know] **Seen on the game server** means a capture saw you on the server that Cav week. It is never ticked for you, because the capture reads the whole week, not one practice.

## Send it

1. Choose **Send staff report**. Once sent, it cannot be changed.
2. Each practice you picked lists you on that section's roll call: as a **Staff guest** in your own platoon or company, and as a **Guest** anywhere else. You never count in that section's numbers.

> [!good-to-know] Your platoon's or company's report shows whether its HQ staff sent theirs, for the leaders above it. Sending your HQ's own report does not stop you sending yours. For a week that has ended, the portal goes by the HQ you were on when it ended, so you still send it after a move.

## Read your staff's reports

1. If you command or read a unit above a platoon or company HQ, the sidebar shows **Staff reports** instead of **Staff report**. It lists every HQ staff member whose report you read, for one week, with your own on top.
2. A PL or PSG reads only their own report and keeps **Staff report**. See [Read your staff's reports](/docs/weekly-report/staff-reports) for the overview.
