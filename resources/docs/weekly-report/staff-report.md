> [!why] Each Cav week HQ staff owe one accountability and one presence. Your staff report is how your chain of command knows you did.
>
> - Accountability: platoon and company HQ staff attend a section practice in their own platoon or company, or an FTX. Battalion HQ staff attend a section practice in the battalion, an operation, a class or another official event.
> - Presence: an hour on the public server, or a one-hour office hour in Discord voice.

## Who sends one

1. Every trooper placed on a platoon, company or battalion HQ: the PL, PSG, CO, XO, 1SG, the battalion command team and anyone else placed there, ACD included. Regiment HQ, staff departments, ELOA, the Reservists and New Recruits send none.
2. You send your own. Nobody can send it for you, not even an Admin.

## Open it

1. Choose **Staff report** in the sidebar, or **Open staff report** on Home. It always opens the running Cav week.
2. You can send it before the week ends. It is due when the unit reports are: Saturday 2359Z, the end of the Saturday after the Cav week.
3. If you have not sent last week's report, the page says so in red, for example "The report for 31OCT26 to 06NOV26 is not sent.", with a link to open that week. You can still send it there, even past its deadline. There is no warning for a week your LOA covered.

## Answer and pick the practices

The form asks one question at a time. The numbered steps at the top show where you are; **Back** and **Next** move between them, and you can go back to any step you have answered.

1. Answer the accountability question with **Yes** or **No**. No skips to the presence question.
2. For Yes, pick what it was: **Section practice** or **FTX** on a platoon or company HQ; **Section practice**, **Operation**, **Class** or **Other official event** on a battalion HQ.
3. For a section practice, pick each one you attended. Only sections in your own platoon or company (for battalion HQ staff, the battalion) that play its game are listed, in order of battle under a heading for each platoon. When the list spans many platoons, only your own starts open; click a platoon's heading to open it. **Find a section** narrows the list and opens every platoon.
   What the form offers follows what counts for your HQ's accountability, set per unit. See [Set what counts for accountability](/docs/your-troopers/accountability-options).
   Each section says where its practice stands next to its name: **Held**, **Not marked yet**, **Marked not held** or **Hasn't happened yet**. A practice can be ticked once its time has passed, whether or not the section has marked it held. Until then it is greyed out. A section with no practice time set has no practice to pick until its leader confirms one was held. The portal refuses a report that claims a practice that hasn't happened yet.
4. For an FTX, an operation, a class or an event, give its name and its date, a day in that Cav week. An FTX may also list the sections that took part.
5. Answer the presence question: **Public server hour**, **Office hour** or **I did not**. Every report needs an answer. For an office hour, give its date, a day in that Cav week, and a short note of what it was, up to 200 characters.
6. Practices already entered for you this week are picked, with who entered them, and the form opens on them. Take off one you did not attend: sending takes it off. One on a practice that hasn't happened yet has to come off before you can send.
7. The last step is **Notes**: write anything command should know about your week, and add **Other events attended** if you like. Both are optional.

> [!good-to-know] If the section marks a practice you claimed as not held, now or later, your report shows **Conflicting information**, for example "Brandt.L reports attending 1/1/A/1-7's practice, but the section marked it as not held." Your report and whether it met the rule stay as you sent them. Your chain of command sees it too.

> [!good-to-know] **Seen on the game server** means a capture saw you on the server that Cav week. It is never ticked for you, because the capture reads the whole week, not one practice.

## Send it

1. The **Your report** card beside the steps (under them on a phone) reads your answer back, shows each picked section as a chip whose x takes it off, and says what is still missing. Choose **Send staff report** there. If you never opened **Notes**, it first asks whether you have nothing to add: choose **Send anyway** or **Go to Notes**. Until it is due you can choose **Edit report**, change it, give a reason and choose **Send again**. After that it is locked: choose **Request edit** and say why, and the leaders above your HQ get it in their bell. If one of them approves, it is unlocked until the coming Saturday 2359Z, and **Edit report** works again until then.
2. Each practice you picked lists you on that section's roll call as a **Staff guest**. You never count in that section's numbers.
3. The portal compares the practices you picked with the game server's capture for that week (CRCON for Hell Let Loose). If a picked practice was captured and none of them saw you, your report is still sent but shows **Self-report conflicts with CRCON data** to you and to your chain of command. When the capture saw you, it says so in green, for example **Seen on CRCON**. With no capture it shows a red **No CRCON data** alert, since your report could not be checked. Link your game identity so the capture can find you. When nothing reads your section's game yet, it says **No game server data: not set up for this game yet**.
4. A public server hour is checked against the game servers marked public for your HQ's game: your time over the whole Cav week is added up. An hour or more shows the minutes in green, for example **75 minutes of server data**. Under an hour shows the minutes the servers found, for example **43 minutes of server data**, even when one of them could not be read. With nothing to compare it says why, for example **No game server data: no public server is set up yet**. The other reasons say that no game account is linked for you, or that the public servers could not be read that week. Your report is sent either way. An office hour is not checked.
5. Some games can never be read automatically, such as Battlefield 6. For those, your report shows no game server alerts. It says once that the server data can't be read for that game and that all attendance is self-reported.
6. Your report is also copied to the regiment's reports sheet. Your report page says **Sent to the sheet** once it is there. When you send it again, its row on the sheet is replaced with the new version.
7. A report sent again lists the versions it replaced under **Earlier versions**. Open one to see what it said, and when and why it was sent again. Whoever reads your report sees them too.

> [!good-to-know] Your unit's report shows whether its HQ staff sent theirs, for the leaders above it. Sending your HQ's own report does not stop you sending yours. For a week that has ended, the portal goes by the HQ you were on when it ended, so you still send it after a move.

## Read your staff's reports

1. If you command or read a unit above a platoon, company or battalion HQ, the sidebar shows **Staff reports** instead of **Staff report**. It lists every HQ staff member whose report you read, for one week, with your own on top.
2. Opening someone else's report also shows their HQ unit's health for that week, as on the overview.
3. A PL or PSG reads only their own report and keeps **Staff report**. See [Read your staff's reports](/docs/weekly-report/staff-reports) for the overview.
