> [!why] Every section in a company posts its rollcall from the company's active template. A mistake in it shows on every section's forum post that week.

## Open a company's template

1. Open **Settings**, then **Templates**, and choose the company.
2. The active template, its versions and a preview are shown. The preview fills itself from one of the company's sections; pick another under **Fill with**.

## Save a new version

1. Edit the template. **Template codes** lists what it can use, such as `{{name}}`.
2. Give a reason and choose **Save as a new version**. A template with a mistake is refused, so it never reaches a rollcall.

## Make a version active

1. Under **Versions**, choose **Make active** on the one to use. **Compare with active** shows what changes.

> [!good-to-know] What this changes, and how to undo it: the next rollcall post in every section of the company uses it. To undo it, make the previous version active again. Nothing is ever overwritten; every save and activation is in the audit log.
