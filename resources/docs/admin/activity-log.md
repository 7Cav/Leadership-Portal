> [!why] When someone reports a bug, the audit log shows what they changed but not what they were looking at. The activity log shows the pages they opened, in order, so you can follow the same steps and see the bug yourself.

## Find their visits

1. Open **Settings**, then **Activity log**. The newest visits are first.
2. Pick the person, and the dates around when the bug happened.

## Read a visit

1. Each row is one page they opened: its address and the time, in Zulu.
2. If they were viewing as someone, or an Admin was acting as someone, the row says who.

> [!good-to-know] Only page addresses and times are kept, never what anyone typed. Sign-in addresses and anything that looks like a token are left out. Visits are deleted after 30 days.
