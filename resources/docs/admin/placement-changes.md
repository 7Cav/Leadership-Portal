> [!why] The portal places troopers from their milpac, so a wrong edit there moves a trooper on the portal too. Someone picks the wrong Gentry, moves them, then puts them back: the portal records both moves. This list shows every move, so you can spot one like that and tell S1.

## Read the list

1. Open **Settings**, then **Org structure**, and choose the **Placement changes** tab. The newest moves are first, for the last 14 days. Choose **7 days** or **30 days** to see less or more.
2. Each row is one move: when, who, from which unit to which, and whether the roster sync or an Admin made it. Moves from one sync share a time.
3. A trooper moved and put straight back has both rows marked. That's usually a wrong edit on the milpac.

> [!good-to-know] A trooper moved and put back inside the same Cav week isn't treated as a transfer: it doesn't excuse their week, and their milpac page doesn't list it. Put back in a later week, both moves stay on their milpac page, marked as a likely correction.
