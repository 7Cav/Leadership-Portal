> [!why] The game server knows players by their in-game name, not their milpac. Once a player is linked, the portal recognises them at every later practice.

## Find the players who need a trooper

1. On a captured practice's roll call, step 2 says how many players need a trooper.
2. Open the panel to see them, each with the troopers whose names are closest. Where the server reports it, a player also shows how long they were on the server during the practice.

## Link a player

1. Open **Choose a trooper** and pick the trooper the player is. The closest names are listed first. To find someone else, type part of their name, such as "Dube.R" or "dube".
2. Choose **Link and mark attended**. This saves their game identity and marks them attended.

> [!good-to-know] If the trooper already has a different ID for that game, the portal asks before replacing it. Choose **Keep** to leave the old one.
>
> A Steam or Epic ID is never replaced by an ID of another kind here. If the trooper really changed platform, change it on the **Game identities** page.

## Players you cannot link

Some players have no **Link and mark attended**, and the panel says why. Mark the trooper by hand instead.

- The game is retired, so it takes no new links.
- The player was seen through BattleMetrics while the game server could not be read, and BattleMetrics only had its own number for them, not their game ID.
- The player is linked to a trooper who has left. They are listed under **Departed** and are not marked on any roll call.

## Dismiss a player who is not a trooper

1. Choose **Dismiss** next to the player. They stay off the list at every later practice.
2. To take a dismissal back, choose **Undo** next to them under **Dismissed**.
