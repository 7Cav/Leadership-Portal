> [!why] A trooper who attends another section's practice gets a makeup for their own section. Recording them here gives them that credit, and counts towards a JSAM if they keep coming.

## Open the guests panel

1. On your section's rollcall, go to step 2, **Section practice**.
2. Find **Guests** below the troopers.

## Add the guest

1. Choose **Add guest**.
2. Type part of the trooper's name and pick them. Only serving troopers from other sections are offered.
3. They are listed as **Added by hand**.

> [!good-to-know] On a past week, their makeup goes to the rollcall that listed them that week, even if they have moved since. Someone on your own rollcall that week cannot be added as a guest.

> [!good-to-know] If the game server already saw them at this practice, they are listed already and cannot be added twice. A trooper who has a staff practice entry for this practice, from before they joined a section, cannot be added either: that entry is on their milpac page. If adding a guest ends in an error, add them again: they are not listed twice, and their makeup is tried again. After a reload, a guest whose makeup is missing says **No makeup on their own rollcall yet** with a **Retry makeup** button that does the same.

## Take a guest back off

1. Choose **Undo** next to a guest you added by hand.
2. The makeup it gave them on their own section's rollcall is taken back, unless their own leader had already marked them. This works even if they have moved to another section since.

> [!good-to-know] A guest the game server saw cannot be removed by hand. Once a practice is confirmed, guests can no longer be added or removed.
