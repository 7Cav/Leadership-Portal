> [!why] A trooper who attends another section's practice gets a make-up for their own section. Recording them here gives them that credit, and counts towards a JSAM if they keep coming.

## Open the guests panel

1. On your section's roll call, go to step 2, **Section practice**.
2. Find **Guests** below the troopers.

## Add the guest

1. Choose **Add guest**.
2. Type part of the trooper's name and pick them. Anyone outside your section can be found, including HQ staff, ELOA, Reservists and troopers no longer serving, who are marked **not serving**.
3. They are listed as **Added by hand**.
4. A trooper from another section gets their make-up on their own section's roll call once your practice is over. Added after it ended, they get it straight away.
5. Anyone without a section roll call that week, such as ELOA, Reservists or someone no longer serving, is listed with no make-up.

> [!good-to-know] On a past week, their make-up goes to the roll call that listed them that week, even if they have moved since. Someone on your own roll call that week cannot be added as a guest.

> [!good-to-know] If the game server already saw them at this practice, they are listed already and cannot be added twice. A trooper who has a staff practice entry for this practice, from before they joined a section, cannot be added either: that entry is on their milpac page. If adding a guest ends in an error, add them again: they are not listed twice, and their make-up is tried again. After a reload, a guest whose make-up is missing says **No make-up on their own roll call yet** with a **Retry make-up** button that does the same.

## Take a guest back off

1. Choose **Undo** next to a guest you added by hand.
2. The make-up it gave them on their own section's roll call is taken back, unless their own leader had already marked them. This works even if they have moved to another section since.

> [!good-to-know] A guest the game server saw cannot be removed by hand. Once a practice is confirmed, guests can no longer be added or removed.

## Staff in the list

1. Staff who attended your practice are listed from their own staff entries: **Staff guest** when your section is in their platoon or company, **Guest** otherwise.
2. They are shown only. They never count in your numbers, get no make-up, and cannot be removed here.
3. You can also add HQ staff yourself with **Add guest**, such as your Platoon Sergeant. They are listed the same way and get no make-up. Their staff report is still theirs to send, and you can take them back off with **Undo**.
