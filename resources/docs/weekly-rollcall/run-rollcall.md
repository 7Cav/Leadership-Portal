> [!why] Your section's weekly report is built from this roll call. A roll call left unfinished leaves your section's numbers wrong in every report above it.

## Who can mark it

1. The SL and ASL mark their own section.
2. Platoon, company, battalion and regiment command can mark every section under them, signed in as themselves. So can an acting lead an Admin named, and Admins.
3. View as is a read-only preview: the gold bar says so and every control is greyed out. Exit view as to mark.
4. To do it in a section leader's place, a leader above them can [act as them](/docs/basics/act-as). It is recorded as you acting for them.

## Open the right section and week

1. Choose **Roll call** in the sidebar. It opens on the section you opened last, or the one you lead. It skips sections with no troopers.
2. To open another section, choose **Switch section** at the top. Sections with no troopers are listed too, marked **No troopers**.
3. Use the arrows beside the week to go back or forward a week, and **This week** to come back. An arrow is flagged when a week it leads to is unfinished.
4. On the Saturday after a week ends, until the report is due at 2359Z, roll call opens on that closing week if your section has not sent its report yet. Choose **This week** for the new week.

> [!good-to-know] A past week lists everyone who was in your section during that week, so you can still finish it after a move or a departure. Those who left say **Moved out** or **Departed**, and **Joined** marks someone who moved in partway through the week. A trooper who joined the Cav after that week is not on it. The current week lists who is in your section now.

## Which steps your week has

The steps are along the top of the roll call. A week follows the steps it started with, so a week that is already under way never changes.

1. **The new roll call**: **Create**, **Post**, **Sign and mark**, **Close for review**. The badge beside the title says where the week stands: **Not created yet**, **Created · not posted**, **Posted · open**, **Ready to close** or **Closed · sent for review**.
2. **A week from before the new roll call**: **Signatures**, **Section practice**, **Review and post**. You then send the report as before.

The sections below cover both. Where a step differs, it says which roll call it is.

## Create the week's roll call

On the new roll call, nothing else opens until the week's roll call is created.

1. In step 1, **Create**, check the **Training date**. It comes from your section's practice slot. Change the day or time to move this week's practice only; the slot stays as it is for later weeks.
2. Check the training topic, the field below the date. It comes from your platoon's topic for the week. When your platoon enforces it, it shows **Set by** and the platoon, and you cannot change it. Otherwise you can change it for your section, or type your own when the platoon has set none.
3. Choose **Create roll call**. If there is no practice this week, choose **Practice will not take place** instead: it cancels this week's practice and creates the roll call.

> [!good-to-know] With no practice slot, the roll call is created without a date; choose **Set the practice slot** to add one. The training date and topic go into the forum post, and the topic is the same one the weekly report shows: change it in either place and both show the change.

## The training date and topic

The card at the top of the roll call shows when practice starts this week and the training topic.

1. Choose **Change** beside the date to move or cancel this week's practice (see [Set the practice slot](#set-the-practice-slot)).
2. Type a new topic and choose **Save**. It is the weekly report's training subject, so the report shows it too. A topic your platoon enforces is locked.
3. If the post is already on the forum, a changed date or topic makes it out of date: copy it again and edit your forum post.

## Record the signatures

1. In step 1, **Signatures** (on the new roll call, step 3, **Sign and mark**), set each trooper to **Will attend**, **Will not attend**, **Make-up requested**, **Excused** or **Unsigned**, as they signed this week's forum thread.
2. Every change saves as you click. If a save fails, the page says your last changes are not saved and keeps them on screen. Choose **Retry**.

> [!good-to-know] Troopers whose leave covers the week are locked and listed under **On LOA**, with the **LOA** button pressed on both tabs from the LOA data. Every other trooper shows the **LOA** button unpressed: it cannot be clicked, it is set only from the LOA thread. They post as LOA; you do not mark them. A trooper whose leave starts next Cav week shows **Leave from** and the date; mark them as usual this week.

> [!good-to-know] **Excused** counts for nothing, like LOA: the week leaves the trooper's RC or SP and is accounted on the report. A trooper's first week in your section after a transfer, and a new recruit's first week, are excused for you: the card shows **Excused, automatic**. Choose any other button to override it.

> [!good-to-know] **Make-up requested** counts as signed. The trooper then shows **Owes a make-up** here and on Home's **Needs attention** until you mark **Make-up complete** for that week, or a host section adds them as a guest.

## Mark section practice

1. In step 2, **Section practice** (on the new roll call, the second list in **Sign and mark**), mark each trooper **Attended**, **Not attended**, **Excused** or **Make-up complete**. A trooper who trained at another section's practice is **Make-up complete**.
2. **Mark all attended** and **Mark the rest not attended** fill many rows at once, and leave an automatically excused trooper alone. Choose **Undo** on the notice to take it back.
3. With the keyboard, J and K or the arrow keys move between troopers. The number keys set the mark: 1 to 5 for signatures, 1 to 4 for practice. On **Sign and mark** they set the kind of card that has focus. Press `?` for **All keyboard shortcuts**.

## Set the practice slot

The header shows when your section practices, in Zulu, with your own time beside it. A section practice is always one hour.

1. Choose the pencil next to the practice time (or next to **No practice slot**).
2. Pick the weekday and the start time in Zulu, then choose **Save slot**. **Remove slot** takes it away.
3. To change one week only, use **This week only**: pick another day and time in the same Cav week and choose **Move this week**, or choose **Cancel this week**. **Back to the usual time** undoes either.

A change or removal never touches a week before the one it starts with: those weeks keep the slot they had. The editor always names the first week it changes. On the current week that is this week, unless the game server already read it or its report was sent (or reopened for editing): it then keeps its time and the new slot starts with the next Cav week, shown in the header as, for example, "Practice Wed 23 59Z from 07NOV26". Opened from a past week, the editor changes the weekly slot from the first week it can, not that past week. This week only cannot be used on a week whose report was sent.

If your section plays a game and has no slot, a warning sits at the top of the roll call. If your game's attendance is read from a game server, it also says why: without a slot, its practices are not captured automatically and make-ups are not given from the game server. Choose **Set the practice slot** on the warning to open the same editor. It goes away once a slot is saved, even one that starts next week.

## Check what the game server saw

1. With a practice slot set, the portal reads the game server by itself within ten minutes of the practice ending. If the game server recorded the practice, the marks are already filled in and say where they came from. A trooper counts as attended after 40 minutes on the server during the hour, unless your battalion set another minimum. Check the marks and fix any that are wrong.
2. Once the practice is over, confirm it: **Confirm practice took place** when it took place and every trooper is marked, or **Practice did not take place** when it did not. Changing a mark by hand afterwards clears the confirmation; confirm again.
3. If the banner says the server could not be read, mark by hand, or choose **Try again** to read it again.
4. Players the server saw who are linked to no trooper are listed for you to link or dismiss. See [Link or dismiss an unmatched player](/docs/weekly-rollcall/unmatched-players).

> [!good-to-know] A trooper with no identity for the section's game has a warning icon by their name, and a warning at the top of the page lists them. The capture cannot see them, so mark them by hand and link them on [Game identities](/docs/your-troopers/game-identities).

> [!good-to-know] Until the practice hour is over, or you confirm it, its marks do not count in your section's numbers and Home does not call it held. The confirm buttons open when the hour ends. A section with no practice slot can confirm any time in the week. Your weekly report cannot be sent until the practice is confirmed as held or not held.

## Guests and make-ups

1. A trooper from another section who came to your practice is a guest. It records **Make-up complete** on their own section's roll call. See [Add a guest to practice](/docs/weekly-rollcall/add-a-guest).
2. With a practice slot set, the game server adds guests by itself: a trooper from another section who was on the server for at least 40 minutes of your practice hour (or the minimum your battalion set) gets **Make-up complete** on their own roll call. A trooper whose own section practises at the same time on the same server is at their own practice, not a make-up.
3. On your own roll call, a make-up the portal recorded says which practice it came from, for example "Make-up at 1/2/B/2-7, seen on the game server". You can change it like any mark, and the portal does not put it back.
4. Staff who say they attended show as **Staff guest**, marked **Staff, not counted here**.
5. A guest added before the practice is over gets their make-up once the practice hour ends.
6. If a guest's make-up did not reach their own roll call, choose **Retry make-up**.
7. Once the practice is confirmed, its guests can no longer change.

## Copy the post and put it on the forum

1. In step 3, **Review and post**, check the changes you made on this visit. On the new roll call this is step 2, **Post**, right after you create the roll call: the marks fill in as the week goes on.
2. Under **Template**, pick the template your section uses: one of your section's own, one your company switched on, the regiment default, or **Company default**. Your pick is kept for later weeks. Templates are made and edited under **Settings**, then **Templates**, in the sidebar; [Roll call templates](/docs/admin/rollcall-templates) says who can edit which and how.
3. Choose **Copy BBCode** and paste it as a reply in the forum's roll call thread yourself. The portal never posts to the forum.

## Mark it posted

1. Once the post is on the forum, choose **Mark roll call posted**. On the new roll call, paste the thread's link under **Forum thread link** if you like (the close step and the report link to it), then choose **Mark as posted, continue**, which takes you on to **Sign and mark**.
2. If you change a mark later, the page says the copied BBCode is out of date. Repost the updated BBCode, then choose **Mark posted again**. A template that uses the live Signed RC and Attended SP images ([Roll call templates](/docs/admin/rollcall-templates) says how) shows the new marks in those images on its own, once the forum reloads them, which can take a minute; the rest of the post is still text you repost.
3. Once the weekly report is sent, the roll call is locked and the page no longer asks for a repost. To change it, edit the report, or choose **Request edit** on it once it is past its deadline ([Send your report](/docs/weekly-report/send-your-report) says how).
4. Then [write and send your report](/docs/weekly-report/send-your-report). On a week from before the new roll call, **Send for review** at the end of **Review and post** opens the report's send window for you.

## Close for review

On the new roll call, closing the week is sending its weekly report.

1. When everyone is signed and marked, confirm the practice, then choose **Continue to close** (or step 4, **Close for review**).
2. Under **A. Update your forum post**, choose **Copy updated BBCode**, edit your roll call post on the forum with it (**Open your forum post** takes you there when you gave its link), then tick **I edited the forum post**.
3. Under **B. Weekly report for Platoon Staff**, check the training and the attendance it will report, write your **Notes to send with this report**, and choose a reason for each trooper who missed practice. They save as you type and are the report's own notes and reasons.
4. Under **Before you close**, check **Practice confirmed**, **Every trooper has a mark**, **Forum post edited** and a reason for each trooper who missed. **Close and send for review** opens once the practice is confirmed.
5. Choose **Close and send for review**. The report opens with its send window: answer any make-up still owed, then send it. See [Write and send your report](/docs/weekly-report/send-your-report).

> [!good-to-know] Once your section's report is sent, that week's roll call is locked: marks, the training topic, the practice confirmation and guests can no longer change. Choosing **Edit report** on the report before it is due opens the roll call again until you send the report again or the deadline passes. On the new roll call you then close it again from **Close for review**. A week too far back is read only too.
