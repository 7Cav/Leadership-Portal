> [!why] Your section's weekly report is built from this rollcall. A rollcall left unfinished leaves your section's numbers wrong in every report above it.

## Open Rollcall and pick your section

1. Choose **Rollcall** in the sidebar. It opens on the section you opened last, or the one you lead. It skips sections with no troopers.
2. To open another section you lead, choose **Switch section** at the top. Sections with no troopers are listed too, marked **No troopers**.
3. To fix a past week, pick it in the week selector. Unfinished weeks are flagged.
4. On the Saturday after a week ends, until your report is due at 23:59 UTC, Rollcall opens on that closing week if your section has not sent its report yet. Choose **This week** for the new week.

> [!good-to-know] A past week lists the troopers who were in your section when that week ended, so you can still finish it after a move or a departure. Their rows say **Moved out** or **Departed**, and **Joined** marks someone who moved in partway through the week. The counts in **Switch section** and on Home follow the same troopers. The current week lists who is in your section now.

## Record the signatures

1. In step 1, **Signatures**, set each trooper to **Will attend**, **Cannot attend** or **Unsigned**, as they signed this week's forum thread.
2. Every change saves as you click.

> [!good-to-know] Troopers whose leave covers the week are locked and listed under **On LOA**. They post as LOA; you do not mark them. A trooper whose leave starts next Cav week shows **Leave from** and the date; mark them as usual this week.

## Mark section practice

1. In step 2, **Section practice**, mark each trooper **Present**, **Missed**, **Makeup** or **Attached**.
2. **Mark all present** and **Mark the rest missed** fill many rows at once. Both can be undone.
3. Number keys mark the trooper in focus. Press `?` for the list.

> [!good-to-know] If the game server recorded the practice, the marks are already filled in and show where they came from. Check them, fix any that are wrong, then choose **Confirm practice**. Changing a mark by hand afterwards clears the confirmation.

## Copy the post and put it on the forum

1. In step 3, **Review and post**, check the changes you made on this visit.
2. Pick the template your section uses: one of your section's own, one your company switched on, or the regiment default. Your pick is kept for later weeks. Your section's own templates are made in [Rollcall templates](/docs/admin/rollcall-templates).
3. Choose **Copy BBCode** and paste it into the forum's rollcall thread yourself. The portal never posts to the forum.
4. If you change a mark after copying, the page tells you the copy is out of date. Copy it again.

## Mark it posted

1. Once the post is on the forum, choose **Mark rollcall posted**.
2. Then [write and send your report](/docs/weekly-report/send-your-report).

> [!good-to-know] Once your section's report is sent, that week's rollcall is locked.
