> [!why] The forum post lists your section the way your fireteams actually run, so the troopers know who they report to on the day.

## Mark the fireteam leaders

1. Open your section's rollcall and find **Fireteam leaders**.
2. Choose the troopers who lead a fireteam.

## Fill the fireteams

1. For each trooper, pick the fireteam they belong to, or **No fireteam role**.
2. Changes save as you go and show in the post on step 3.

## Choose how the post shows them

1. Choose whether fireteam leaders are **Shown** or **Hidden** in the post.
2. Choose where they go: above their own fireteam, or **All fireteam leaders above the rest**.

## Who may change what

1. A section's leaders set its fireteam leaders, fireteams and display options.
2. Company command can set one rule for every section in the company: show or hide fireteam leaders, list them first, and whether the SL and ASL may lead a fireteam. While a company rule is set, the sections' own choices for it show **Set by company** and cannot be changed.
3. To let each section choose again, company command sets the rule back to **Each section chooses**.
