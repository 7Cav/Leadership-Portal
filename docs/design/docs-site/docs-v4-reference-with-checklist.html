Weekly rollcall

Run this week's rollcall

Record who attended practice, confirm the list, and mark it posted so your platoon leader sees it in the report.

Every leadership billetAbout 5 minutesOnce a week
Why this matters. The weekly report is built from your confirmed rollcall. An unconfirmed rollcall leaves your section blank in the roll-up.

1Open Rollcall and pick your section

Choose Rollcall in the sidebar. You land on your own section. If you lead more than one, use the section picker at the top of the page.

1Screenshot: Rollcall page, section picker

2Mark each trooper

Set every trooper to present, excused, or absent. Troopers on approved leave are already marked for you.

2Screenshot: attendance list

3Check unmatched players

Players seen in the game who aren't linked to a trooper appear in their own list. Link each one to a trooper, or dismiss them.

Not sure who someone is? See Link or dismiss an unmatched player.

4Confirm, then mark it posted

Select Confirm when the list is right. After you've posted it on the forum, select Mark as posted.
